A time-pressure cooking game set in a pink-and-gold Paris pâtisserie.
Guests pin cake orders to the brass rail — Fraisier Classique,
Mille-Feuille Lumière, Gâteau Myrtille, Chocolat Royal, Opéra Lumière —
and you build each one by hand: stack the layers in the ticket's exact
order, send the génoise sponge through the oven, finish with the named
piping flourish, and serve before the guest's patience runs out.

## How to play

- **Stack** — click an ingredient bin (or press `1`–`8`) to lay it on the
  assembly plate. Layers must match the ticket, bottom first.
- **Bake** — génoise sponge goes on raw. Click **BAKE SPONGE** (or `B`)
  and watch the oven timer; serving raw sponge fails the ticket.
- **Pipe** — finish at the piping bench: Rosettes (`Q`), Zigzag (`W`) or
  Swirls (`E`). The ticket names the finish it wants.
- **Serve** — click **SERVE** (or `Space`) to hand the plate to the
  matching ticket. Fast service earns a tip on top of the price.
- **Bin** — `Backspace` scraps a ruined plate so you can start again.
- Patience bars drain on every ticket; a guest who walks out is a strike.
  Three strikes — or the end of the 3-minute shift — closes the atelier.
  Every 45 seconds the rush deepens: patience drains faster, guests
  arrive sooner, and the grander cakes join the menu.

## Running it

Open `game/index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder with
`node serve.mjs` and visit http://127.0.0.1:8416/.

Deterministic screenshot scenes are available at
`?shot=cover|rush|serve&seed=7`.
